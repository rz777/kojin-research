How much power is possible?

The reference Kojin collection reaches approximately 93.373M March Combat Power at the investigated maximum: all relevant research completed, VIP 15, optimized roles, five developed March Commanders, maximum Flame gear and Army Attack +20%.

Every variant uses exactly 260,000 Imperial Drake Rider, matching the permanent March Size at Castle 25. Army Size Boosts are excluded. The 260,000 troops are a scenario assumption; the reference account held only one unit at the snapshot.

Why not simply choose the five Kojin with the highest power?

At 260,000 T5, one percentage point of Attack bonus is worth 83,200 power. Moving from +3% to +8% therefore adds 416,000 troop power. Even a high raw Kojin power value often cannot offset that difference. The optimization evaluates every eligible Main/Manager pair, then selects the five largest contributions of 8,320,000 × Cavalry Attack bonus + Kojin power.

A larger city bonus may consume a valuable commander. That is why city and march roles were optimized together, rather than assigning the same best Kojin independently to every role.

Does Kojin level matter?

Yes for direct Kojin power; no for the same native Cavalry Attack percentage. A Kojin with +8% Cavalry Attack retains that bonus while leveling. As a March Commander, it adds more raw power at higher levels. As Main or manager, its raw power is not added in this march model.

Direct Kojin power calculation
effectiveLevel = min(Kojin Level, Player Level, 60)
blend = 0.65 × (effectiveLevel − 1) / 59 + 0.35 × PromotionStep / 15
rarityFactor = startingFactor + (maximumFactor − startingFactor) × blend
Kojin Power = roundToOneDecimal(6 × levelBase × promotionMultiplier × rarityFactor)

At Level 60, levelBase is 8,510. Promotion P5.3 reaches a multiplier of 1.65 and also increases the rarity component. Therefore, simply multiplying the unpromoted value by 1.65 is insufficient.

What could enable 100 million with 260,000 troops?

With the collection’s native Kojin traits unchanged, all investigated existing-collection variants remain below 100 million. Even with gear, a boost, VIP 15, full research and promotion, the gap is 6,626,937.5. Leveling alone cannot close it: the selected commanders are already fully developed in this scenario.

The strongest additional opportunity is a new collection with higher matching Cavalry Attack traits. Five matching native stars correspond to +40%, compared with the existing maximum of +8%. Leveling or promotion does not turn +8% into +40%.

A concrete theoretical comparison: seven different Legendary Kojin, each with five matching Cavalry Attack Stars. Two fill the Main and Cavalry Attack Manager roles; five are March Commanders at Level 60, P5.3. This gives a modeled city multiplier of 1.40 × 1.40 = 1.96, commander bonus +200% and raw commander power of 1,263,735.

Priorities for the reference account

  1. Secure the 260,000 T5 and suitable commanders. Select percentage traits first; raw Kojin power is the next consideration.
  2. Develop research selectively. Moving from R 2.436 to 9.70 adds 60,436,480 power. Maximum research is the ceiling, but not automatically the most cost-effective next step.
  3. Keep the existing Main role during preparation. Full reassignment yields only a small combat improvement and can sacrifice useful research bonuses.
  4. Choose gear according to the effort required. Maximum Elite L5 Flame takes substantially more investment than smaller sets.
  5. Level and promote commanders. This adds power without changing native Attack percentages. Leveling the Main or Attack Manager solely for that percentage bonus does not help this march.
  6. Activate the Army Attack item when deploying. An Army Size item is not useful for this fixed 260,000-troop scenario.
  7. For more than the collection maximum, investigate stronger matching traits. Compare prices and actual bonuses, not only rarity or level.

Formula and model assumptions

B = 260,000 × 32 = 8,320,000
M = research multiplier + (city multiplier − 1) + commander bonus
    + VIP bonus + Hunter Gear bonus + Army Attack item + other active bonuses
March Combat Power = floor(10 × B × M) / 10 + min(commander power, B)

For the full collection maximum: M = 9.70 + 0.1664 + 0.35 + 0.10 + 0.60 + 0.20 = 11.1164. Therefore: 8,320,000 × 11.1164 + 884,614.5 = 93,373,062.5.
