Exactly how power becomes damage

The calculations below describe opening army power, boss-specific damage and the cap imposed by remaining boss HP.

B = Σ (remaining troop countᵢ × unit powerᵢ) W = Σ (remaining troop countᵢ × unit powerᵢ × boss modifierᵢ) K = min(B, commanderPower) Neutral damage = floor(B × troopMultiplier + K) Potential boss damage = floor(W × troopMultiplier + K) Useful damage = min(remaining boss HP, potential boss damage)
Troop tierT1T2T3T4T5
Raw power per troop, all four families14122432

Kojin activation is capped by unboosted troop power. Commander power is added once, without the boss counter multiplier. This is why a ×2 counter does not quite double total displayed army damage. The formation response provides a single troopMultiplier; this scalar is applied after the family-weighted raw-power sum, including for mixed formations.

Overkill does not spill into the next boss. Only the remaining HP can count as useful damage. Attack power is recalculated from surviving troops, so five attacks do not automatically equal five times the opening power. The client returns whole-number damage with Math.floor; server formation previews can retain one decimal in initialPower.

Concrete example: a 260,000-troop reference formation

B = 3,244,420 · M = 2.6814786189211017 · K = 76,520.1 Neutral damage = floor(B × M + K) = 8,776,362 Vampire: W = B + siege raw power 761,328 Potential damage = 10,817,847 Haunted Knight: W = B + 15% × archer raw power 761,340 Potential damage = 9,082,590

The reference formation has 8,776,362.9 initial power, giving 8,776,362 neutral damage after rounding down. This is an opening-power calculation, not a battle outcome or a route clear.

RULES Injury percentage is based on the original committed troop count, not the shrinking remainder. Any strictly positive rounded matchup bonus reduces that injury rate by 20%. The useful-damage / potential-damage ratio then scales casualties down when the hit overkills the boss.

matchupPercent = round((potentialDamage / neutralDamage − 1) × 100) rate = difficulty.injuryPercent / 100 × (matchupPercent > 0 ? 0.8 : 1) × (usefulDamage / potentialDamage) woundBudget = originalTroopCount × rate + previousInjuryRemainder wounded = min(remainingTroopCount, floor(woundBudget + 0.000000001))

Wounds are distributed proportionally by surviving troop count, using floors plus largest fractional remainders; ties use troop-type name order. The fractional injury remainder is retained. This is not distribution by troop power or troop tier. A nominal counter bonus below the 0.5-percentage-point rounding threshold will not trigger the 20% injury reduction.

Each leader-requested rescue adds two attacks per player and halves both reward weights again. Rescue attacks do not increase the efficiency allowance. The live leaderboard’s efficiency ordering uses difficulty.rewardWeight × efficiency / 2^rescues, after placing completed guilds first. The exact server efficiency numerator/denominator and payout rounding are not exposed.

Ghost bonus: the active boss panel advertises a 1% chance to win the Ghost March skin when attacking Ghost. It is optional and unnecessary to unlock the final boss. A translation string mentions that retries do not grant another roll; the drop implementation and independence of repeated attacks are not exposed. Do not infer a five-roll or cumulative drop probability from the 1% label.

Finalization: rewards are finalized after the event; recorded CROWN is separate from wallet delivery. No current row has a payout. Season-level shard / eligible-Kojin multiplier text is also visible in the parent event page, but no extra such multiplier appears in the inspected raid damage or reward-weight calculations; this report does not insert it into the raid formula.
