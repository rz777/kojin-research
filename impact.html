For the optimal march, Kojin C must return first; Kojin F and Kojin G must be released from their existing roles. Main Kojin G also provides Research Speed +8% at the snapshot. While continuing research, it makes sense to keep that economic bonus and switch the Main only for combat. Main/Manager levels do not increase their native Attack traits.

Model checks and limitations

At the snapshot, the reference account had no promotions and no Hunter Gear. Reconstructed raw power across all 181 Kojin matched the recorded subtotal of 1,234,222.2.

A formation preview using 390,000 hypothetical T5 cavalry, no commanders and no active items returned 32,428,032 at a multiplier of 2.5984. It confirms the research, VIP and city-bonus inputs at the snapshot. Accepting a hypothetical troop count does not confirm ownership or deployment availability. The optimized role-and-boost scenario is a model built on those inputs.
